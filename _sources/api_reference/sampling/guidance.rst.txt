.. _api_guidance:

Guidance
========

Guidance recipes for conditional sampling. Each recipe returns the per-modality
:py:class:`Generator <stix.core.generator.Generator>` consumed by
:py:class:`Solver <stix.sampling.solver.Solver>` and
:py:class:`ManualSolver <stix.sampling.solver_manual.ManualSolver>` via their
``guidance_fn`` config.
